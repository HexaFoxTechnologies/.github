## Why "HexaFox"?

We met at Sri Ramakrishna Institute of Technology, Coimbatore, in a hostel room that wasn't really ours,
then spent our last two years of college in a rented house together. Our group chat has always been
called **Nanga Nari Kootam**, Tamil for "we're a pack of foxes".

**Hexa** for the six of us, like six sides of one shape. **Fox** for the pack: clever, curious and quick to adapt.
Our mark blends the Tamil letter **ஆ** (from *ஆறு*, "six") with a fox.
